Daniel: The Man in Babylon

Book Two · From the series The Believers Series

Faithful in an empire built to erase him — and he never once bent.

Daniel was likely a teenager when he was taken captive to Babylon, stripped of his name, his language, and every marker of his identity, and placed at the center of an empire built to remake him into something else entirely.

Daniel: The Man in Babylon follows his decades-long life in a foreign court — long before the lions' den made him famous — tracing a faith that held not through a single miracle, but through years of quiet, unglamorous refusal to bend.
