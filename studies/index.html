A Personal Study

Sermon Studies

William Branham left behind 1,206 recorded sermons preached between 1947 and 1965. This is my own slow, sermon-by-sermon study of that body of work — close readings, historical context, and reflection questions, written up as I read and listen through them. It's not part of my author catalog; it's personal study I'm sharing as I go, in whatever order I happen to get to a given sermon. The master index below tracks every sermon that exists, so the full scope of the project is visible from day one — whether I've studied ten of them or a thousand.

57-0417 · April 17, 1957

The Second Coming Of The Lord

A homecoming sermon at Branham's own tabernacle, reading an eyewitness earthquake, Israel, and the Cold War's atomic clock as signs of the end.

 47-1102 · November 2, 1947

The Angel Of God

Branham's own account of the room, the star, and the figure who commissioned his healing ministry — plus two claims of foreknowledge he invited the crowd to test on the spot.

 47-1100X · Circa November 1947

Fellowship

A rare, informal recording from Arizona — trumpet solos, hog-hunting stories, and a few words from Branham's wife Meda. Not a sermon, but worth studying anyway.

 47-0412 · April 12, 1947

Faith Is The Substance

Branham's case that faith isn't a feeling you work up, but a direct, positive kind of knowing — a "sixth sense" as real as sight or touch.
