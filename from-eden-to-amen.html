Tracing God's design from the first word to the last.

From Eden To Amen steps back from any single figure or event to ask a larger question: does scripture actually function as one coherent story, or is that unity something readers impose after the fact?

Tracing patterns from Genesis through Revelation, this book makes the case that the Bible's design is intentional — a single story told across dozens of authors and centuries, ending exactly where it began.
