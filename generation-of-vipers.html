Generation of Vipers: The Seed of the Serpent in Scripture

Buy from retailer

Amazon

What kind of serpent talks? And what does a piece of fruit have to do with being naked?

These aren't rhetorical questions — they're the two threads Scripture leaves dangling in the very first pages of Genesis, threads that most readers rush past on their way to the more familiar parts of the story. But look closer, and the ground beneath Eden's fall starts to shift.

Generation of Vipers traces the seed of the serpent from that first garden all the way to the present hour — through the prophets, through the Gospels, through the exact words Jesus himself used to name it. This is not a comfortable study. It is a careful one.
