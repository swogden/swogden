Salt: Remember Lot's Wife

Buy from retailer

Amazon

One glance back, and a warning carried in a pillar of salt for three thousand years.

Genesis gives her no name — only a warning and a single backward glance. Salt imagines the woman married to Lot in the last hours before Sodom's destruction: what she knew, what she couldn't leave behind, and why looking back cost her everything.

Faithful to the handful of verses scripture actually gives us, Salt fills the silence between the lines without ever contradicting the text — a biblical fiction built on reverence, not speculation for its own sake.
