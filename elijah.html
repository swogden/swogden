Elijah: The Still Small Voice

Book One · From the series The Believers Series

He walked into the throne room of the most powerful king in Israel with no name, no credentials, no gift — and words that stopped the rain.

Elijah appears in scripture with no introduction and no genealogy — simply a man from Gilead who tells a king it will not rain again except by his word. What follows is one of the most dramatic prophetic careers in the Hebrew scriptures: fire from heaven, a raven-fed exile, and a contest on Carmel that ends four hundred prophets.

But the real turn of the story comes after the triumph — not on the mountain of fire, but in the cave where God speaks not in wind, earthquake, or flame, but in a still small voice. Elijah: The Still Small Voice traces the full arc of a prophet undone by victory and remade in silence.
