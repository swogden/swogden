BEHOLD: The Hidden Prophecy in God's Name

Buy from retailer

Amazon

A name that has carried prophecy since before the burning bush.

Long before Exodus 3, the name of God was already present in scripture — hidden, some argue, in the genealogy of Genesis five, spelled out across the meanings of ten names most readers skip past entirely.

BEHOLD examines this claim carefully: what the Hebrew names actually mean, what the evidence supports, and what it would mean if God's own name had been prophetically embedded in the text since before the flood.
